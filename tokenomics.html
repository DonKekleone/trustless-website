$TRUSTLESS Tokenomics

Fixed supply: 100 million $TRUSTLESS. Clear allocations ensure transparent growth and long-term incentives.

Total Supply
100,000,000

Launch Float

~49% at launch (35% presale + up to 14% liquidity). The remaining 51% vests over ~24 months with milestone-based unlocks.

Liquidity Provision Note

Up to 14% of supply, or ~40% of presale funds raised, will seed the liquidity pool. If presale does not sell out, unsold tokens from this allocation will be permanently burned.
