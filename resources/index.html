Why It Matters

From blind trust to visible rules.

Most online transactions still depend on one side taking the risk first. The buyer sends money and hopes. The seller delivers work and hopes. Trustless payment systems reduce that dependency through transparent rules, committed funds and verifiable settlement.

Trustless Payments explores the technology, economics and operating models behind that shift.
