Glossary

Terms used across Trustless Payments.

A reference for common concepts used throughout Trustless products and documentation.

Escrow

A payment arrangement where funds are held or governed until defined conditions are met.

Milestone

A discrete deliverable or stage with its own payment conditions.

Settlement

The final movement of funds according to the applicable payment rules.

Destination Tag

An XRP Ledger value used by some workflows to associate payments with the correct destination or agreement.

Memo

Optional transaction metadata that can help identify or describe an XRP Ledger payment.

Contract State

The current lifecycle stage that determines which actions are valid.
