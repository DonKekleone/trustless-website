Crypto Freelance Pricing Guide

Price freelance work in crypto without leaving the exchange rate undefined.

A project can be priced directly in a crypto asset or priced in fiat and settled with crypto. Those are different obligations. The contract should specify the unit of account and the exact moment any conversion becomes binding.

Frequently asked questions

How to Price Freelance Work in Crypto

Should freelance work be priced in crypto or fiat?

Either can work, but the agreement must clearly identify the unit of account and any conversion rule.

When should the crypto exchange rate be calculated?

Use a named source and specific event, such as contract funding, with a defined quote window.

Who carries crypto price volatility?

That depends on whether the obligation is fixed in crypto or fiat and when conversion becomes binding.

Should each milestone use its own conversion rate?

It can. Separate conversion at each funding event may reduce long-term exposure when the rule is written clearly.
