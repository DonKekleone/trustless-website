Milestone Rejection Guide

A client rejected the milestone. Now test the rejection against the scope.

Rejection should identify a requirement the delivery failed to meet. It should not become an unlimited veto, a new creative brief or a way to avoid paying for work that satisfies the funded agreement.

Require a specific reason

A useful rejection points to the failed acceptance criterion.

The client should identify the missing file, incorrect function, unsupported format or other written requirement. General disappointment cannot tell the provider what must be corrected.

Quote the relevant requirement.

Attach evidence of the failure.

Separate defects from preferences.

Submit feedback inside the review window.

Compare delivery with scope

Review the version of the agreement that was funded.

Private messages, later ideas and unstated assumptions should not replace the recorded deliverables. Check exclusions and client responsibilities alongside the promised outputs.

Verify the delivered files and links.

Review stated exclusions.

Confirm client inputs arrived on time.

Preserve the original funded scope.

Apply the revision clause

Included revisions correct or refine work within scope.

The provider should receive consolidated feedback and the agreed revision period. A request for another feature, audience, platform or creative direction belongs in a new milestone.

Use one consolidated feedback set.

Respect the included revision count.

Set the revision deadline.

Price material additions separately.

Reach a defined outcome

The process needs an end state.

After a compliant revision, the client reviews against the same criteria. The agreement should define approval, release, refund, expiry or escalation without allowing review to continue indefinitely.

Re-test the written criteria.

Release accepted work promptly.

Use refund rules only when applicable.

Record the final decision.

Frequently asked questions

What to Do When a Client Rejects a Freelance Milestone

Can a client reject a freelance milestone for any reason?

A fair rejection should relate to the written scope and acceptance criteria rather than an unstated preference.

What should milestone rejection feedback include?

It should identify the failed requirement, explain the evidence and state the correction requested within scope.

Does rejection mean the client gets a refund?

Not automatically. Revisions, review timing and settlement follow the agreement’s rules.

Is a new feature an included revision?

Usually not. New functionality outside the funded scope should become separately priced work.
