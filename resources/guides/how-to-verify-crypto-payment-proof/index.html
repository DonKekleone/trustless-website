Crypto Payment Verification Guide

Verify crypto payment proof from authoritative ledger facts before value, access or work is released.

Verify crypto payment proof by checking the transaction on the relevant public ledger—not by trusting a screenshot, wallet notification or copied hash. On XRPL, validated status, successful result, destination, delivered amount, asset, Destination Tag and memo context must agree with the expected payment.

Automation controls

Make verification strict enough to fail safely.

An automated verifier should reject or quarantine ambiguous payments instead of guessing. Normalize expected values, guard against duplicate hashes, verify the correct network and record the exact checks performed. When attribution fails, preserve funds and route the case for controlled review. Never let memo text override a wrong destination or insufficient amount. Safe failure prevents one malformed payment from activating the wrong contract.

Frequently asked questions

Verify Crypto Payment Proof Before a Screenshot Tricks You Into Releasing Work or Funds

Is a crypto payment screenshot proof of payment?

No. Screenshots can be edited or unrelated; verify the transaction directly against public ledger data.

What fields should I check on a crypto payment?

Check validated status, successful result, destination, delivered amount, asset and any required tag or memo.

Does a transaction hash prove I was paid?

Only if the referenced transaction is valid and its fields match the expected payment.

Should work begin while a payment is pending?

Wait until the required transaction has successfully settled and the agreement recognizes the funding.
