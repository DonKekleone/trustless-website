Freelance Contract Examples

Freelance acceptance criteria turn taste and expectations into reviewable requirements.

Acceptance criteria define the evidence a client uses to approve a milestone. Strong criteria describe observable outputs while leaving subjective preferences to references and revision rules.

Design work

Specify the assets, sizes, formats and included concepts.

A design milestone might require three initial concepts, one selected direction, two revision rounds and final PNG, SVG and source files at stated dimensions.

Name the number of concepts.

List output dimensions and formats.

Identify required editable source files.

Define included revision rounds.

Development work

Describe behavior that can be demonstrated or tested.

A development milestone should identify supported devices, required routes, form behavior, integrations, error states and the environment where acceptance testing occurs.

Use feature-level requirements.

Name browser or device support.

Define test credentials and data.

Separate defects from new features.

Writing and video

Use countable specifications and delivery formats.

Writing criteria can state length, audience, outline, sources and file format. Video criteria can state duration, aspect ratio, resolution, captions, audio and source-project delivery.

Set a reasonable length range.

Provide required references and tone.

Define technical export settings.

List captions, thumbnails or source files separately.

Avoid subjective traps

Quality words need supporting evidence.

Professional, viral, beautiful and engaging do not create reliable pass-or-fail tests. Use references to guide taste and objective criteria to determine whether the promised deliverable exists.

Do not guarantee audience reactions.

Do not confuse preference with defect.

Use revision rules for reasonable refinement.

Approve against the funded scope.

Frequently asked questions

Freelance Acceptance Criteria Examples

What are acceptance criteria?

They are the observable requirements used to determine whether a deliverable satisfies the agreed milestone.

Should acceptance criteria be measurable?

Whenever possible. Formats, quantities, functions and compatibility are easier to review than subjective adjectives.

Is client satisfaction valid acceptance criteria?

Satisfaction alone is too open-ended. Pair creative references with concrete deliverables and finite revision rules.

What happens when criteria are missing?

The parties may disagree about completion because the contract does not provide a shared test.
