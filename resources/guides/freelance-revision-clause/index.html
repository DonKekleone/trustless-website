Freelance Contract Guide

A freelance revision clause protects the client’s result without making the provider’s labor infinite.

Freelance revision clause language defines how delivered work may be corrected or refined before an escrow milestone settles. It should state the included rounds, feedback method, response deadline and boundary where a revision becomes separately priced scope.

Define a revision

A revision changes delivered work within the original scope.

Correcting a color, sentence or supported function may fit the funded deliverable. Requesting a second brand, new page or different campaign is added scope even when the client calls it a revision.

Tie requests to the accepted scope.

Distinguish defects from preferences.

Group feedback into one round.

Price material additions separately.

Limit the rounds

Finite revisions protect price and schedule.

State the number of included rounds and what counts as one round. Consolidated feedback avoids a stream of isolated requests being treated as separate unlimited obligations.

Specify one or two included rounds.

Require one consolidated request per round.

Both parties need a predictable response period.

The client should request an included revision during the review window. The provider then receives a stated period to deliver it. Silence and missed deadlines should lead to known contract states.

Revision anatomy

Define the permitted action instead of promising vague flexibility.

State how many rounds are included, what one round contains, when feedback is due and how long the provider receives to respond. Require requests to remain within the funded scope and acceptance criteria. A freelance revision clause should explain the consequence of missed clocks rather than leaving settlement suspended indefinitely.

Set a finite round count.

Require written consolidated feedback.

Define both response clocks.

Price additional rounds in advance.

One round

A round is one consolidated decision followed by one updated delivery.

Without this definition, five messages from different stakeholders can become five moving targets while still being called one revision. Assign one client representative, collect internal comments and submit a single prioritized request. Conflicting instructions should pause the revision clock until the client supplies one authoritative direction.

Choose one feedback owner.

Consolidate stakeholder comments.

Resolve contradictions before work resumes.

Record the revised delivery.

Defect or addition

Correction preserves the promised result; added scope changes it.

Fixing a broken agreed function or omitted deliverable belongs inside the original obligation. Adding another page, format, campaign, audience or concept does not. Compare the request with the saved agreement and prevent scope creep by quoting new value and time before performing it.

Tie defects to acceptance criteria.

Do not rename expansion as revision.

Quote new objectives separately.

Preserve the original funded record.

Escrow timing

Review and revision clocks must lead back to settlement.

After delivery, the client approves or submits a valid included request within the review period. The provider responds within the revision period, and the client reviews against the same criteria. The contract lifecycle should expose extensions, silence and missed obligations so neither party can freeze escrow through inactivity.

Start clocks from recorded events.

Limit extensions and record them.

Keep the original acceptance test.

Define silence and missed-deadline outcomes.

Closing leverage

Completed work should not remain collateral for a brand-new negotiation.

When included revisions satisfy the funded rules, release that milestone. Additional ideas can become another agreement without blocking earned payment. This protects clients from defective delivery while preventing endless preference changes from consuming the provider’s margin. The strongest clause makes both protection and finality visible before anyone commits money or labor.

Release satisfied milestones.

Move new ideas into new scope.

Do not hold unrelated stages hostage.

Make finality part of the original bargain.

Frequently asked questions

Freelance Revision Clause: Protect Quality Without Opening the Door to Unlimited Unpaid Work

What is a freelance revision clause?

It defines how many revisions are included, how feedback is submitted, when revisions are due and what counts as additional scope.

How many revisions should a freelancer include?

The right number depends on the service, but one or two clearly defined rounds are common for fixed-scope work.

What counts as a revision round?

Usually one consolidated set of feedback followed by one updated delivery.

Is a new feature a revision?

No. A new deliverable or function outside the original scope should generally be priced as additional work.
