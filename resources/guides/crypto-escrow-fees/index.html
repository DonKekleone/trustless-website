What fees support

Escrow involves more than moving an asset between wallets.

A payment workflow may create the agreement, identify funding, maintain contract state, record actions, route settlement and support the infrastructure that keeps the process available.

How percentage fees work

Calculate the fee from the funded amount.

If a platform charges 5.89%, a 100 XRP funded contract produces a 5.89 XRP platform fee under the stated fee model. Confirm whether the displayed contract amount is gross, net or exclusive of fees before funding.

100 XRP × 5.89% = 5.89 XRP.

500 XRP × 5.89% = 29.45 XRP.

1,000 XRP × 5.89% = 58.90 XRP.

Fee versus exposure

The cheapest payment rail is not always the cheapest failed deal.

Direct payment may have minimal transaction cost while exposing one party to the full amount. Escrow cost should be compared with the probability and impact of non-delivery, nonpayment or an unresolved disagreement.

Consider the amount at risk.

Consider counterparty history.

Consider project length and complexity.

Use smaller milestones to limit concentrated exposure.

Questions to ask

Understand the fee before signing or funding.

Check when the fee is charged, who economically bears it, whether network costs are separate, how refunds are treated and whether partner or operational routing changes the user-facing amount.

Frequently asked questions

Crypto Escrow Fees Explained

How much does crypto escrow cost?

Fees vary by platform and transaction. Trustless Payments publishes its current platform fee and worked examples on the Fees page.

What is 5.89% of 100 XRP?

It is 5.89 XRP.

Are escrow fees the same as blockchain fees?

Not necessarily. A platform fee and the network transaction fee may be separate costs.

Is escrow worth the fee?

That depends on transaction size, counterparty risk and the value of verified funding, milestones and defined settlement rules.
