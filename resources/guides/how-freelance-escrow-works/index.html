Freelance Payment Guide

How freelance escrow works for clients and independent workers.

Freelance escrow gives both parties evidence before either takes the full risk. The client commits payment; the freelancer completes a defined deliverable; settlement follows the agreement.

Before work begins

Convert a conversation into a fundable agreement.

A useful contract identifies both parties, the exact work, the amount, the delivery period, the review window and the revision allowance. Funding an unclear promise only locks ambiguity into the transaction.

Name the actual files, functions or outputs.

State the asset and amount being paid.

Set delivery, review and revision deadlines.

Define release, refund and cancellation conditions.

Funded milestones

A funded milestone is evidence that payment has been committed.

The client deposits the amount assigned to a stage before the freelancer performs it. The freelancer can confirm the money exists without receiving it before delivery.

Fund only work both parties understand.

Use smaller stages for longer projects.

Give each milestone an observable completion test.

Do not begin from a screenshot or promise of later funding.

Delivery and review

Review should test the written agreement, not invent a new one.

The freelancer submits the promised work. The client checks it during the stated review period and either approves it or requests an included revision tied to the acceptance criteria.

Keep delivery evidence attached to the agreement.

Separate defects from new feature requests.

Record extensions and decisions.

Release completed stages instead of holding unrelated work.

Completion and reputation

A resolved contract leaves more than a payment receipt.

Settlement closes the financial obligation. Reviews and recorded completion can then provide future counterparties with evidence that is stronger than a disposable username or private testimonial.

Release work that satisfied its milestone.

Refund genuinely unused stages according to the rules.

Close remaining obligations clearly.

Leave accurate, transaction-based feedback.

Frequently asked questions

How Freelance Escrow Works

What does freelance escrow mean?

It means client funds are committed under an agreement and released to the freelancer after defined work and review conditions are met.

Should a freelancer start before escrow is funded?

Starting before verified funding restores the nonpayment risk that escrow is meant to reduce.

Who controls freelance escrow funds?

Control depends on the system, but neither party should be able to ignore the settlement rules stated before funding.

Should every project use milestones?

Small assignments may need only one stage. Larger or uncertain projects usually benefit from several independently reviewable milestones.
