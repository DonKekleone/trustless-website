Step 2 · Prepare an XRPL wallet

Use a compatible self-custody wallet carefully.

This guide uses Xaman because it supports XRPL accounts, trustlines and transaction signing. Other compatible wallets may also work.

Install from an official listing

Avoid wallet links delivered through unsolicited messages or search advertisements. Confirm the developer and application listing.

Protect the account secret

Generate and store recovery credentials privately. Never send a seed, family seed, secret or private key to Trustless Payments, a promoter, support account or trading interface.

Activate and fund the account

Send enough XRP to activate the account and cover the intended trade, applicable account reserves, trustline requirements and transaction costs.

Self-custody means responsibility. Losing recovery credentials, signing a malicious transaction or sending funds to the wrong address can cause permanent loss.
Step 3 · Add the trustline

A trustline authorizes your account to hold one issued asset.

The trustline must identify both the TRLS currency and the official issuer. Review the transaction inside your wallet before signing.

  1. Open the official trustline flow

    Use the prepared XRPL Services link or the trustline flow supplied by the verified TRLS/XRP market.

  2. Confirm the currency

    The asset should identify TRLS using its XRPL currency representation.

  3. Confirm the issuer

    Match the full issuer address: r9NcytRxC1CX1vERGn9HGR2Bwbuo6tGX1h.

  4. Review the limit and transaction

    Understand what the trustline transaction authorizes before approving it in the wallet.

  5. Sign only inside the wallet

    The external page may prepare a transaction, but secret credentials should remain inside the wallet’s signing process.

Step 4 · Review the market

A live market is not a guaranteed exit.

The TRLS/XRP market can be accessed through an XRPL trading interface. Before signing, inspect the asset pair, issuer, order type, quantity, estimated execution and current liquidity.

Market order

A market-style trade prioritizes execution against available offers. In a thin market, the resulting price may differ substantially from the most visible quoted price.

Limit order

A limit order specifies acceptable terms but may remain unfilled, partially fill or never execute if the market does not reach them.

Liquidity

Displayed price does not show how much value can trade near that price. Examine order depth and expected execution before signing.

Issuer verification

Confirm the TRLS issuer again on the proposed trade. A familiar ticker does not prove the order uses the official asset.

Step 5 · Confirm the result

Verify the signed transaction and resulting balance.

After signing, confirm that the transaction reached a validated ledger, succeeded and affected the intended account and asset.

Transaction status

Confirm that the transaction is validated and successful rather than relying on a wallet animation or pending message.

Correct account

Make sure the trustline and resulting balance belong to the XRPL account you intended to use.

Correct issuer

Verify that the balance remains associated with the official TRLS issuer address—not merely an asset displaying the same ticker.

Recorded order outcome

Confirm whether the trade filled completely, partially or remained open. Signing an order does not guarantee immediate full execution.

Risk disclosure

TRLS is a utility token—not a promise of profit.

Buying, holding or providing liquidity involves material risk. Review the system and make an independent decision before signing.

Price and liquidity risk

Market value can change sharply. Available liquidity may be insufficient to buy or sell the desired amount near a quoted price.

Issuer and asset risk

Issued XRPL assets have different properties and issuer relationships than native XRP. Understand the exact asset being held.

Wallet and signing risk

Compromised credentials, malicious links and misunderstood transactions can cause permanent loss.

Platform and technology risk

Wallets, interfaces, applications and infrastructure can contain defects, become unavailable or change their behavior.

This guide is educational and operational. It is not financial, legal or investment advice, and it does not guarantee market access, liquidity, utility, price performance or returns.
