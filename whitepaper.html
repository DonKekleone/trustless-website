Abstract

Trustless Payments is an escrow automation system for XRPL. Creators define objective conditions (e.g., market‑cap target within a deadline). Funds are held until conditions are met. If satisfied, funds release; if not, they refund. No middlemen, no negotiation—just code enforcing outcomes.

Economically, the system funds development through a 5.89% escrow fee, split 50% to development operations and 50% to automatic market buy‑and‑burn of the associated token, reinforcing a level playing field.

1. Problem

  • Trust failure: Hand‑shake promises, subjective milestones, and social pressure produce fraud and drama.
  • Coordination tax: Human arbitration adds delays, bias, and costs.
  • Perverse incentives: Rent‑seekers thrive; honest builders bear risk.

2. Solution: Enforced Escrow

Replace trust with verifiable conditions and machine enforcement. The Trustless Telegram bot walks users through escrow creation, funding, and automated resolution. Conditions can reference measurable on‑chain or off‑chain data (e.g., token market cap from reputable sources) and are evaluated on a schedule until expiry.

Create: Amount, recipient, contract address, target market cap, expiry (days).
Fund: Send exact XRP with bot‑provided memo/tag. Post TX hash; the bot detects funding.
Resolve: If target met before deadline → release; else → refund.

3. Mechanics

  • Escrow records: Stored with unique memo/tag, status timeline, and immutable audit fields.
  • Condition checks: A scheduler evaluates goals against data sources at intervals until resolution.
  • Determinism: State transitions are rule‑based and logged.
  • Compatibility: Built for XRPL; architecture permits multi‑chain expansion.

Implementation note: for MVP we standardize deadlines as N days and use market‑cap whole numbers. Advanced condition templates are planned.

4. Fees & Buy‑Burn

  • Escrow creation fee: 5.89%
  • Split: 50% to dev operations / 50% to automated market buy‑and‑burn of the associated token.
  • NFT holder target fee: ~1% (planned mechanics).

Every split and burn is observable on‑chain. No private ledgers. No hidden allocations.

$TRLS aligns incentives with product usage. Demand from fees → buy‑and‑burn loop reduces circulating supply over time while funding continued development.

6. Security & Risks

  • Key handling: Users never share private keys with the bot. All transfers are user‑signed on XRPL interfaces of their choice.
  • Data integrity: Market‑cap feeds are validated and sanity‑checked; failures trigger safe fallback behavior.
  • Irreversibility: On‑chain actions cannot be reversed. Users must verify addresses, tags, and amounts.
  • Known limitations (MVP): Condition templates intentionally simplified (days, whole‑number caps) to minimize complexity and attack surface.

7. Governance & NFTs

Governance begins utility‑first: ship core enforcement, then expand influence. The planned Trustless Toads NFT collection provides fee reductions and community signaling. Token‑weighted and NFT‑gated proposals may be introduced after security reviews.

8. Roadmap

  • MVP: Telegram flow, escrow creation, funding detection, periodic condition checks, release/refund, fee split + buy‑burn.
  • Hardening: Monitoring loop reliability, data source redundancy, incident logging.
  • Expansion: Advanced condition templates, multi‑chain support, permissionless developer extensions.
