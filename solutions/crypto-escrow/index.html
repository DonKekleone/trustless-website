Why Escrow

Holding funds is useful. Structuring the outcome is better.

Crypto escrow reduces the need to send assets directly to a stranger and hope the other side performs. Trustless workflows add visible conditions, verifiable funding and defined settlement logic.

Verified funding

Confirm that the expected value was committed through public XRP Ledger activity.

Visible conditions

Establish amounts, deadlines, review periods and settlement rules before risk changes hands.

Rule-based release

Release value according to the contract state instead of an informal promise.

Defined refund paths

Identify when eligible funds can return before disagreement begins.

FAQ

Crypto Escrow, explained.

What is crypto escrow?

Crypto escrow separates digital assets from direct counterparty control until defined conditions are satisfied.

What makes an escrow trustless?

A trustless design reduces dependence on personal discretion through visible rules and verifiable transaction state.

Can crypto escrow prevent every dispute?

No. It can reduce payment risk, but it cannot guarantee quality or eliminate unclear scope.

Why use XRPL for escrow workflows?

XRPL provides fast settlement and independently reviewable transaction records for verification.
