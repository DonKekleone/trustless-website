A Better Deal

The client should not pay blindly. The freelancer should not work on a promise.

Informal DMs usually force one side to accept all the risk. Structured freelancer payments replace that imbalance with funded contracts, measurable deliverables and defined settlement rules.

Funded commitment

The freelancer can verify that money has been committed before beginning the work.

Defined deliverables

Each milestone can state what must be delivered, when it is due and how it will be reviewed.

Review and revisions

Clients receive a defined review window instead of an unlimited or improvised approval process.

Release or refund

Funds follow the agreement state rather than whoever becomes louder in the final DM.

FAQ

Freelancer Payments in XRP, explained.

Can I pay a freelancer in XRP?

Yes. Trustless Network structures XRP payments around funded agreements, milestones and settlement rules.

Does the freelancer know the contract is funded?

Funding can be verified against public XRP Ledger activity before work begins.

Can a project use more than one milestone?

Yes. Larger projects can be divided into separately defined and settled deliverables.

Does escrow guarantee good work?

No. Escrow reduces payment risk, but clear scope, evidence and realistic deliverables still matter.
