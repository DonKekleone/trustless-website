Controlled Exposure

Do not bet the entire project on one final handoff.

Milestone payments divide scope, capital and approval into smaller stages. The client gains checkpoints. The provider gains a path to incremental payment instead of waiting until the entire project ends.

Smaller commitments

Limit the value and labor exposed during any single stage of the agreement.

Independent deliverables

Give each milestone its own scope, deadline and reviewable outcome.

Incremental settlement

Release completed work without waiting for the entire project to finish.

Defined progression

Move through work, review, revisions and settlement using explicit contract state.

FAQ

Milestone Payments, explained.

What is a milestone payment?

A milestone payment is tied to a defined stage or deliverable inside a larger agreement.

Why use milestones instead of one payment?

Milestones reduce the amount of money and labor exposed at any single point.

Can each milestone have its own deadline?

Yes. Each stage can define its own delivery and review expectations.

What happens after a milestone is approved?

The applicable payment can be released and the agreement can progress to the next stage.
