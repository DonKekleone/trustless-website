FAQ

XRP Payments, explained.

Can businesses accept XRP payments?

Yes. XRP can serve as the payment rail while application logic manages contract and settlement context.

Why are destination tags and memos important?

They help associate a ledger transaction with the correct user, contract or funding instruction.

Are XRP transactions publicly verifiable?

Yes. Transaction records can be independently reviewed on the XRP Ledger.

Is an XRP payment automatically an escrow payment?

No. Escrow requires additional conditions or workflow logic governing release and refund.
