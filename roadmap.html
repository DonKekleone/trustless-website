Phase 0 — The Awakening

The concept takes form.
The Trustless Bot is born — a mechanism to hold all parties accountable, no matter their reputation.
Development begins on XRPL, the most battle-tested foundation for fast, final settlement.
The rules are simple: if conditions are met, payment executes. If not, it doesn’t.

The early code is raw but alive.
It will evolve, automate, and one day — judge.

Phase 1 — Enforcer Bot (XRPL Core)

Condition-based escrow logic comes online.
Market cap or time-based releases. Immutable logic. No negotiation.
The Enforcer doesn’t rely on reputation — only math.

This phase brings automated escrow creation, fund detection, and condition monitoring — built directly on XRPL rails.
A transparent fee model funds development and token burn, with every transaction reinforcing the economic loop.
The field is being leveled.

Phase 2 — Trustless Token ($TRLS) Expansion

$TRLS becomes the backbone of the ecosystem — a utility, a signal, a vote of confidence.
It powers escrow creation fees, community governance, and liquidity.

All team, marketing, and development tokens unlock slowly over time — no sudden windfalls, no rug mechanics.
Each month, only a small fraction of supply is released as progress continues.
Unused allocations may be burned — permanently tightening supply.

This isn’t inflation. It’s proof of longevity.
We’re building for two years minimum — and beyond that, for those who carry the torch.
