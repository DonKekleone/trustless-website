Operating Principles

Code replaces trust.

Trustless systems are designed around deterministic rules and verifiable activity rather than informal promises.

Proof over promises.
Funding, settlement and payment activity can be verified on-chain.
Rules are freedom.
Clear terms reduce ambiguity for both sides of an agreement.
A level playing field.
The same contract logic applies regardless of reputation, status or personal influence.
Honor through automation.
Good agreements should not depend on one party voluntarily doing the right thing after conditions have already been met.
How Funds Move

Agreement

Funding

Settlement
