About

TRUSTLESS is a mechanism, not a promise.

When trust breaks, systems fail. This token replaces human discretion with code—transparent, verifiable, and automatic. It ensures outcomes without relying on reputation or authority.

In doing so, it helps establish a level playing field, where honest actors are protected and agreements are enforced by design, not belief.
