Contract Rules

The agreement should be clear before funding.

Trustless contracts use defined states, timers and valid actions to reduce ambiguity during payment workflows.

Funding

An agreement becomes funded only after the expected ledger activity is verified.

Delivery

Work can be governed by an explicit delivery period or milestone deadline.

Review

Clients may receive a defined review period before settlement behavior advances.

Revisions

Where revisions are included, their number and timing can be defined in advance.

Extensions

Extension requests and decisions can be recorded as contract actions.

Settlement

Release or refund behavior depends on contract state and applicable rules.
