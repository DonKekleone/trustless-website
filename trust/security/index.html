Trust & Security

XRPL escrow security starts with verifiable behavior.

Trustless Payments is designed to reduce ambiguity around funding, contract state and settlement while making critical payment activity independently reviewable.

Ledger verification Explicit payment instructions Defined contract state Visible settlement history
Principles

Reduce trust assumptions wherever possible.

Verify funding

Payment state should be tied to ledger activity rather than screenshots, messages or unsupported claims.

Make rules visible

Contract terms, deadlines and valid actions should remain understandable throughout the agreement lifecycle.

Separate identity and payment state

Application identity and ledger transactions are related but distinct parts of the system.

Operational Safety

Users still have responsibilities.

Automation reduces some forms of risk, but it does not eliminate the need to verify addresses, payment instructions and contract terms.

Check destination details

Confirm the destination address, amount, destination tag and memo requirements before sending funds.

Review contract terms

Understand deadlines, review windows, revision rules and settlement behavior before funding an agreement.

Protect wallet credentials

Never share seed phrases, private keys or wallet recovery credentials with Trustless Payments or another participant.

Verify on-chain activity

Where appropriate, independently review the relevant transaction on the XRP Ledger.

Transparency

Understand the system before moving funds.
