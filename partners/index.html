Partner FAQ

Understand the program before you promote it.

How long does referral attribution last?

Referral attribution is stored on the visitor's device for 90 days. The visitor must connect an eligible wallet during that window for the wallet to become associated with the referring partner.

Does the partner earn for only 90 days?

No. The 90-day period is the window for connecting and attributing the wallet. Under the current routing system, a successfully attributed wallet can continue producing partner fees when it funds additional qualifying contracts.

How much does the partner receive?

The partner receives 25% of the platform fee collected from qualifying attributed activity. With the current 5.89% platform fee, that equals 1.4725% of the qualifying funded amount.

When are partner fees paid?

Qualifying partner shares are routed automatically in real time when the applicable platform fee is processed.

Can one referred wallet generate more than one payment?

Yes. If an attributed wallet funds multiple qualifying contracts, each qualifying transaction can produce an additional partner fee.

Where do I find my partner link?

Connect your wallet to Trustless Network, open the Account page and use the affiliate portal to access and copy your referral link.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. Earnings depend entirely on qualifying funded activity generated by attributed wallets. Traffic, clicks and wallet connections alone do not guarantee compensation.

Can the program rules change?

Yes. Fee percentages, eligibility requirements, qualifying activity and routing mechanics may evolve as the software and network develop. Current rules apply at the time qualifying activity occurs.
