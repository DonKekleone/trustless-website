Contract Lifecycle

From agreement to settlement.

The network is designed to keep both sides inside a clear sequence of actions instead of relying on informal promises.

Create

Define the parties, title, payment amount, deadlines, review period, revisions and milestone structure.

Fund

Funding is verified against XRP Ledger activity before the agreement enters its active state.

Work

The operator completes the deliverable while the contract clock and defined rules remain visible to both parties.

Review

The client reviews submitted work inside a defined review window and can approve or request revisions where permitted.

Revise

Revision requests follow the contract's configured revision rules and deadlines.

Settle

Funds are released or refunded according to the agreement state, recorded actions and enforcement rules.

Built on XRPL
