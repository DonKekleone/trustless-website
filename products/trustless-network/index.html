Fairer Deals By Design

Neither side should have to surrender first.

Buyers should not have to send money and hope. Providers should not have to finish the work and hope. Trustless Network puts visible rules, committed funds and reputation between both sides.

Agreements

Execution

Reputation

Build reputation

Contract Lifecycle

Rules replace guesswork.

Every deal moves through a visible sequence of actions, deadlines and conditions so neither side has to rely on vague promises once money or work is committed.

Create

Define the parties, payment, deadlines, review windows, revisions and milestone conditions before the deal begins.

Fund

Funds are committed and verified before the agreement becomes active, giving both sides proof that the deal is funded.

Work

Work happens while deadlines, milestone terms and contract rules stay visible to both sides.

Review

The client reviews submitted work inside a defined window and can approve it or request revisions according to the agreement.

Revise

Revision requests follow the deal's predefined limits, deadlines and conditions instead of becoming an open-ended negotiation.

Settle

Funds are released or refunded according to the agreement, recorded actions and enforcement rules — not whoever argues harder afterward.

Who It Is For

Fairness looks different from each side.

Keep leverage without withholding commitment

Clients can fund the deal without handing control to a stranger. Define the terms upfront and release value according to the agreement.

Know the money is there before you work

Providers can take on work with visible funding, clear deadlines and defined review rules instead of chasing payment afterward.

Bring productive relationships into the network

Partners can introduce buyers and providers, share referral links and participate in activity created through attributed relationships.

Enforcement

Rules stay visible after the deal begins.

Deadlines stay real

Delivery, review and revision periods remain part of the active agreement instead of disappearing into chat history.

Decisions leave a record

Extensions, approvals, denials and other contract actions can be recorded inside the workroom.

Payment follows the agreement

Approved milestones can settle according to the contract lifecycle and configured payment rules.

Protection works both ways

Where contract conditions require it, remaining funds can be returned according to the agreement state.
