Economic Transparency

Do not take the claim. Inspect the evidence.

TRLS supply, company wallets, escrow locks, liquidity activity and fee transactions exist on the XRP Ledger. Where the ledger can provide evidence, the ledger should be the source of truth.

Public ledger Visible escrows Inspectable wallets Documented routing
What Can Be Checked

Transparency should produce receipts.

Fixed supply

TRLS has a stated fixed supply of 100,000,000 tokens on the XRP Ledger.

Company wallets

Operational allocations are separated into designated wallets for marketing, growth, development, community and treasury functions.

Escrow locks

Most company-held tokens are secured in multi-year XRPL escrows with ledger-visible conditions and release dates.

Liquidity activity

TRLS/XRP liquidity positions and related transactions can be inspected through XRP Ledger data.

Fee routing

The published model assigns four equal shares to partners or marketing, liquidity, LP rewards, and operations.

Reward distributions

Completed on-chain distributions can be compared with the rules published for the applicable participation period.

Limits of Transparency

The ledger proves transactions—not intentions.

On-chain records can verify balances, escrows and transfers. They cannot guarantee future product demand, token value, uninterrupted operations or the success of any economic program.

What documentation adds

Public documentation explains the intended purpose of each wallet and program. Ledger records show what actually happened. Both should be reviewed together.
