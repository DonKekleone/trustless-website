TRLS Tokenomics

100 million tokens. Every allocation has an address.

TRLS has a fixed supply of 100,000,000 tokens on the XRP Ledger. The supply is divided among six designated allocation categories, with the issuer and operational wallets published for independent review.

The Math

The entire supply is accounted for.

5M liquidity + 23M marketing + 21M growth + 19M development + 15M community + 17M treasury = 100M TRLS.

The issuer address identifies the asset. It is not an additional allocation on top of the 100,000,000-token supply.

Multi-Year Locks

Most company-held supply is restricted through XRPL escrows.

Multi-year escrow objects restrict access until their ledger-defined release conditions are satisfied. Tokens moved into escrow no longer appear as ordinary spendable balances in the originating wallet.

That is why current wallet balances should be reviewed together with escrow objects and transaction history—not compared mechanically with the original allocation table.

Allocation is the plan. The ledger is the receipt.

The allocation table explains each category's intended responsibility. Bithomp and other XRPL explorers show wallet activity, balances, transfers and escrow records.
