TRLS Tokenomics

Fixed supply. Assigned responsibilities.

TRLS has a fixed supply of 100,000,000 tokens. Company-controlled supply is divided among designated wallets and largely secured through multi-year XRPL escrows.

100M fixed supply No additional issuance Designated wallets Multi-year escrows
Allocation Structure

Each wallet has a job.

The supply is separated by operational purpose so marketing, development, community programs and treasury planning do not become one undefined pile.

Initial liquidity

The initial market allocation established TRLS/XRP liquidity and circulating supply.

Marketing

Reserved for distribution, campaigns, partnerships and bringing real users into Trustless products.

Growth & expansion

Reserved for liquidity expansion, integrations and long-term ecosystem growth.

Development & infrastructure

Supports engineering, hosting, security, maintenance and continued product development.

Community & loyalty

Supports documented rewards, participation programs and useful network contributions.

Strategic treasury

Provides operational runway, stability and the ability to act on long-term opportunities.

Time Locks

Most company-held tokens are locked for years.

Long-term XRPL escrows restrict access until their specified release dates. The locks exist on the ledger and can be inspected independently.

Why it matters

A label on a wallet is a statement. An on-chain escrow is a restriction. The economic model uses both: documented responsibilities and ledger-enforced time locks.

Operating Principle

Release should follow purpose—not convenience.

Treasury tokens are intended to enter circulation deliberately through liquidity, development, rewards, partnerships and measurable ecosystem activity. The treasury is operating infrastructure, not a substitute for product revenue.
