Economic Participation

Rewards for supporting useful activity.

Trustless rewards are built around contribution. Partners help distribute the products. Liquidity providers help support the market. Each role has a separate economic path.

Activity-based LP-token eligibility Partner attribution No guaranteed returns
LP Participation

Support liquidity. Participate in the economic activity.

Twenty-five percent of collected platform fees is allocated to rewards for eligible TRLS/XRP LP-token holders.

Eligibility

Participation is based on holding eligible TRLS/XRP LP tokens under the rules published for the applicable distribution period.

Funding source

The reward bucket comes from collected platform fees—not from an advertised fixed yield or an unlimited inflation schedule.

Documented mechanics

Eligibility rules, timing and completed distributions should be documented so participants can inspect what occurred.

Partner Rewards

Bring qualifying activity. Participate in the fees.

A separate 25% fee bucket is routed to the attributed partner when a qualifying wallet uses Trustless Network. When no partner is attached, that bucket supports marketing.

Two roles. Two reward paths.

Partners are rewarded for distribution. Eligible LP-token holders are rewarded for supporting liquidity. Holding one role does not silently substitute for the requirements of the other.
