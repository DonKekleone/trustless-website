Platform Fees

One fee. Four equal jobs.

Trustless Network applies a 5.89% platform fee to applicable contract payments. Every collected fee is divided into four equal economic buckets.

5.89% platform fee Four equal buckets 25% of fees each 1.4725% of payment each
Fee Routing

Every quarter of the fee has a defined purpose.

Each bucket receives 25% of the collected 5.89% fee. That equals 1.4725% of the underlying payment per bucket.

25% of collected fees

Partners & marketing

Routed to the attributed partner when qualifying activity comes through a partner link. Without an attributed partner, this share supports marketing.

25% of collected fees

Liquidity strengthening

Reserved for strengthening TRLS/XRP liquidity and supporting healthier market infrastructure.

25% of collected fees

LP-token rewards

Allocated to participation rewards for eligible holders of TRLS/XRP LP tokens under the applicable program rules.

25% of collected fees

Operations & staff

Funds infrastructure, maintenance, continued development, administration and the people required to operate the products.

Example: 100 XRP payment

A 100 XRP applicable payment produces a 5.89 XRP platform fee. Each of the four buckets receives 1.4725 XRP.

What “25%” means

The 25% figure describes each bucket’s share of the collected platform fee. It does not mean the customer is charged a 25% platform fee.

Partner Distribution

Distribution has a defined seat in the model.

Partners can receive one full 25% fee bucket from qualifying attributed activity. The routing is part of the economic design—not an afterthought.
