What TRLS Is

The economic layer—not the product pretending to be one.

Trustless Payments is the company. Trustless Network and Enforcer are products. TRLS is the utility and participation layer surrounding selected activity across that ecosystem.

The products must remain useful without requiring a token story to justify their existence. TRLS earns its role by connecting to identifiable actions, liquidity and contribution.

Token Consumption

Burns connect utility with scarcity.

When an applicable network action requires TRLS to be burned, the utility event permanently removes those tokens from circulation.

The objective is not destruction for spectacle. The objective is a visible relationship between product activity and token consumption.

Activity Loop

Use → burn → permanent receipt.

A qualifying product action creates the reason for the burn. XRP Ledger activity creates evidence that the burn occurred.

TRLS/XRP Liquidity

A utility token needs a market people can use.

TRLS/XRP liquidity connects the token with an active XRP market. Liquidity providers supply assets to the automated market maker and receive LP tokens representing their position.

Market access

Liquidity supports the ability to move between TRLS and XRP through the applicable automated market maker.

Price discovery

Pool activity provides an active market rather than a price supported only by promotional claims.

LP position

LP Participation

One LP position. Two fee streams.

TRLS/XRP liquidity positions can participate in the fee mechanics of the underlying automated market maker.

25% Fee Bucket

Platform activity can reward liquidity support.

Twenty-five percent of collected platform fees is allocated to rewards for eligible TRLS/XRP LP-token holders under the applicable participation rules.

Platform Economics

Product usage feeds four operating functions.

Applicable Trustless Network payments include a 5.89% platform fee. Every collected fee is divided into four equal buckets.

25%

Partners & marketing

Routed to an attributed partner for qualifying activity or used to support marketing when no partner is attached.

25%

Liquidity strengthening

Reserved for strengthening the TRLS/XRP liquidity layer and related market infrastructure.

25%

LP-token rewards

Allocated to participation rewards for eligible TRLS/XRP LP-token holders.

25%

Operations & staff

Supports infrastructure, maintenance, administration, contributors and continued development.
