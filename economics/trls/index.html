Network Economics

Product activity creates economic activity.

TRLS is the fixed-supply utility token of the Trustless ecosystem. It connects network actions, liquidity and participation to products people can actually use.

XRPL token 100,000,000 fixed supply Activity-based utility Ledger-visible
The Role of TRLS

The token supports the products. The products do not exist to support the token.

Trustless Payments builds payment infrastructure. Trustless Network and Enforcer create the activity. TRLS supports selected utility, liquidity and participation functions around that activity.

Utility

Used inside the network

Selected actions—including Signals and other participation mechanics—can require TRLS or consume it through burns.

Liquidity

Backed by an active market

Part of the platform fee model is dedicated to strengthening TRLS/XRP liquidity as product activity grows.

Participation

Useful contribution matters

Partners, liquidity providers and network contributors can participate through documented programs tied to measurable activity.

Economic Engine

Every collected platform fee is divided four ways.

The 5.89% platform fee supports distribution, liquidity, LP participation and the people and infrastructure required to keep building.

25% of collected fees

Partners & marketing

An attributed partner receives this share for introducing qualifying activity. When no partner is attached, the share supports marketing.

25% of collected fees

Liquidity strengthening

This share is reserved for strengthening TRLS/XRP liquidity and improving the economic infrastructure surrounding the token.

25% of collected fees

LP-token rewards

This share supports documented participation rewards for eligible holders of TRLS/XRP LP tokens.

25% of collected fees

Operations & staff

This share keeps the infrastructure online, pays contributors and funds continued product development.

Supply Structure

Fixed supply. Divided responsibilities. Long-term locks.

TRLS has a fixed supply of 100,000,000 tokens. Company-held allocations are divided among designated operational wallets, with most of those tokens placed into multi-year XRPL escrows.
