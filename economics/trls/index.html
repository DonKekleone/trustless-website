Role

TRLS is infrastructure utility, not the company identity.

Trustless Payments is the company. Trustless Network and Enforcer are products. TRLS supports selected economic functions across the ecosystem.

Signals

Publishing utility

Selected network actions can require TRLS burns, connecting token use to actual platform activity.

Messaging

Interaction utility

TRLS can be used as a participation mechanism for selected communication or network actions.

Incentives

Rewards & participation

The ecosystem can use TRLS to support rewards, operator incentives and other participation programs.

Supply

Fixed supply: 100,000,000 TRLS.

Allocation, liquidity, locked wallets and ecosystem reserves are intended to remain visible and documented.

Utility

TRLS use is designed around actions inside the Trustless ecosystem rather than serving as a substitute for product usefulness.

Transparency

Token supply, liquidity and designated ecosystem wallets can be reviewed through XRP Ledger activity.

Current Utility

Designed to be consumed by real network actions.

Signals

Publishing a Signal can require a TRLS burn, creating a direct relationship between network publishing activity and token consumption.

Messages

Messaging utility can use TRLS as an access or participation mechanism, depending on the active product configuration.

Operator incentives

TRLS may be used to reward useful network participation, onboarding, contests and ecosystem contributions.

Future utility

Additional utility may be introduced as the Trustless product ecosystem grows. New functions should remain tied to identifiable network activity.
