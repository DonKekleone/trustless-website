How to Buy $TRLS

Five quick steps: install Xaman, create & fund your wallet, add the trustline, and trade on Sologenic.

Step 1 — Install Xaman (XRPL Wallet)

Download the official mobile wallet to hold XRP and interact with the XRPL DEX.

Google Play App Store

Step 2 — Create Wallet (Safety First)

  • Generate your seed/secret in private. Never share it.
  • Optional: turn on Airplane Mode while writing the seed, then re-enable internet.
  • Store the seed offline (paper/steel). Don’t screenshot or email it.

Step 3 — Fund with XRP

Send XRP to your Xaman address from an exchange or another wallet. Keep a little extra for network fees.
